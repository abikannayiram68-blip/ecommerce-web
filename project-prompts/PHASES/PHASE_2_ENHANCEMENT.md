# PHASE 2: Enhancement — Product Discovery & Customer Engagement

## Objective
Improve product discoverability and increase customer engagement through intelligent search, wishlists, reviews, notifications, promotions, and personalized recommendations.

## REQ-IDs Covered
REQ-007, REQ-016, REQ-017, REQ-018, REQ-019, REQ-020, REQ-021, REQ-022, REQ-030

## Deliverables
- PostgreSQL full-text and trigram search (typo-tolerant)
- Wishlist management (add, remove, move to cart)
- Product reviews and ratings (submit, moderate, display)
- Recently viewed products tracking
- Notification center (BullMQ background jobs)
- Promotional banners and offers
- Admin promotions management
- Personalized product recommendations
- Smart suggestions engine (wishlist, history, purchases, trending)
- Similar product suggestions and trending sections
- Redis caching for search and recommendations

## Success Metrics
- Increased search-to-product-click rate
- Higher wishlist adoption
- Improved customer engagement and repeat visits
- Measurable recommendation-driven revenue

# PHASE 3: Marketplace — Multi-Vendor & Advanced Commerce

## Objective
Transform the platform into a multi-vendor marketplace enabling third-party sellers to register, list products, and manage their own storefronts while the platform owner takes commission.

## Deliverables
- Seller registration and onboarding

- Multi-vendor product management
- Commission and fee calculation engine
- Payout and settlement system
- Vendor-specific storefronts
- Marketplace-level admin controls
- Vendor performance analytics
- Dispute resolution system
- Review system extended (per-vendor ratings)
- Advanced search (vendor filter, location-based)
- Multi-currency support
- International tax handling
- Vendor messaging system

- 50+ active sellers onboarded
- Marketplace GMV growth
- Seller retention rate
- Buyer trust and satisfaction
